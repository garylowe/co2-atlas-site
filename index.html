Common questions

Where does the data come from?
Monthly flask measurements and global means come from the NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory (gml.noaa.gov). Carbon-13 values come from the Scripps CO₂ Program. Fire and volcano events come from NASA's Earth Observatory Natural Event Tracker (EONET), and satellite map layers from NASA GIBS. The app downloads them directly and does not alter the values.

Why is a station missing recent months?
Flask samples are shipped to Boulder for analysis, so NOAA usually publishes them several months after collection. Some stations have gaps when sampling paused.

Does a wildfire near a station explain its reading?
Not necessarily. The app shows events and station anomalies together so you can compare them, but monthly flask data is too coarse to attribute a change to one event. The cards say this where it matters.

Does the app work offline?
After the first load, downloaded data is cached on your device, so you can browse it without a connection. The map tiles need a connection.
