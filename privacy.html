Privacy policy

CO₂ Atlas · Last updated 30 October 2026

In short: CO₂ Atlas does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

Data the app downloads

To show measurements and events, the app requests public data files directly from:

  • NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory (gml.noaa.gov), for atmospheric CO₂ measurements
  • NASA Earth Observatory Natural Event Tracker (eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov), for wildfire and volcano events
  • NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov), for satellite map layers, only when you turn one on
  • Scripps CO₂ Program, UC San Diego (keelinglabsites.ucsd.edu), for carbon-13 measurements at some stations

These requests contain no personal information. As with any internet request, those servers receive your IP address and basic request details; their handling of that is covered by their own policies (NOAA, NASA, UC San Diego).

Maps

The map is drawn with Apple Maps through Apple's MapKit framework. Map tiles are requested from Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not request or use your location.

Storage on your device

Downloaded data is cached on your device so the app loads faster and works offline. The cache contains only the public data described above. The latest global mean is also stored for the app's home screen widget, if you add it. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
