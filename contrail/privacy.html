Privacy policy

Contrail Tracker · Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version
Contrail Tracker has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don’t receive or store any of your data.

Your location

With your permission, the app reads your location while it is open. It sends the coordinates to the services below to fetch the aircraft and weather above you. It never uses your location in the background, and we never receive or keep it. You can choose a place by search or on the map instead of sharing your location.

  • adsb.lol: live aircraft positions near you
  • OpenSky Network: backup aircraft positions, and recent flights if you add an account
  • Open-Meteo: weather at flight level for your position
  • Apple Maps: the map and place search

Aircraft details and routes are looked up from adsbdb.com and hexdb.io using the aircraft’s public identifier, not your location. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy, and like any internet request they receive your IP address.

OpenSky account (optional)

If you add an OpenSky Network client ID and secret, they are stored in your device’s keychain and are only ever sent to OpenSky to sign in. We never see them. You can remove them in Settings at any time.

Motion and fitness

The app never reads motion or fitness data. iOS lists a motion permission because a location component inside the app is able to; the app does not ask for it.

Settings stored on your device

Your radar range and data-source choices are saved on your device only, and are deleted if you delete the app.

Children

Contrail Tracker is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
